I. Studio Visit

Many hands. Two chairs. One door.

About the studio Maisonneuve has been Étienne's project since 2018 — shaped by the practice that two chairs is enough.

It is impossible to walk past 5360 Avenue du Parc and not notice the door. It is painted in the colour Étienne calls "le rouge des autobus" — the red of the city buses — and during the snow months it is the only door on the block that doesn't fade into the slush.

Inside, the shop is two chairs, a single basin, a record player that plays Charles Aznavour on Tuesdays and Sonic Youth on Fridays, and a wood-burning stove that Étienne installed himself in the second winter. There is no front desk. The list is on a clipboard.

"The list is a clipboard because the day we get an iPad is the day the shop changes — and the shop is not going to change."

A profile in GQ Style — written before Étienne would talk to anyone — ran in the spring of 2023. The list filled for the rest of the year within a week. Étienne has not added a third chair. He has been asked. He has declined.
