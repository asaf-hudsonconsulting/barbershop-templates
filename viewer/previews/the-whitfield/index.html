The House

James Henderson was appointed to a Mayfair barber at nineteen and to a hotel on Madison at twenty-six. He opened on 73rd Street in 1994 with two chairs, a basin from a closed Madison Avenue hotel, and the same straight razor he was given on his last day in London.

Thirty years on, the chair by the window is kept Saturday mornings for Mr. Pellegrino. The Sinatra is the same. The shave is the same. The discerning gentleman is welcome.
