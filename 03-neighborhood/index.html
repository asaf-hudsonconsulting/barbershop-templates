The shop on Third Ave.

Pete's father cut hair in Bensonhurst for 38 years. When Pete opened on 3rd Avenue in 2004, he brought his dad's old basin and the chair from Chair Three — that's the one closest to the window.

The shop's busiest hour is Saturday at 11am, when fathers bring sons before the football game starts. The TV's on ESPN unless the Yankees are on. Don't ask us about the Mets.

Mr. Esposito has been in every other Wednesday for nineteen years. Same cut. He knows the rest of the schedule too — ask him.
