How it works

Built for the way you actually book. Available today.

Sasha left a thirteen-year salon career in 2021 to build a barbershop on first principles: one client, one barber, one standing slot every six weeks.

The result: less time on the small talk, more time on the cut. Lower no-show rate. Better hair. The kind of result you feel before you can describe.
