The Story

Three generations on Nine Mile

Lou Domanski opened the shop in March 1956 with a chair he carried in from his uncle's place in Hamtramck. The lease was $42 a month. The Tigers were three weeks from opening day.

Lou cut hair every Tuesday through Saturday for forty-six years. His son took over in 2002. His grandson Anthony bought it in 2019. Anthony kept the radio, the chair, and the Detroit pennants on the wall.

Mr. Kowalski has been coming in every other Wednesday since 1971. We still cut his hair the same way Lou did — short on the sides, longer on top, parted on the left. Same combs, mostly. Same talc.
